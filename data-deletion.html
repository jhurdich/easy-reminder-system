User Data Deletion Instructions

To request deletion of your Easy Reminder System account and associated reminders:

  1. Email aslgospellearning@gmail.com from the email address connected to your account.
  2. Use the subject line “Easy Reminder System data deletion.”
  3. Include your name and the sign-in email address you want deleted. Do not include your password.

We will verify the request, delete the account information and reminders associated with it, and confirm completion. Requests are normally completed within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law.

You may also revoke Easy Reminder System’s access from your Google or Facebook account settings. Revoking access does not itself delete data already stored in Easy Reminder System, so please email the request as described above.
