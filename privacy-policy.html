Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Easy Reminder System helps people create and manage personal reminders. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we may receive your name, email address, profile identifier, and profile image, as provided by that service. We also store the reminders and settings you choose to save.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, keep your reminders associated with your account, provide the service, and send reminder-related messages when you request them. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use Firebase Authentication and Firestore to provide sign-in and secure data storage. Google and Facebook process sign-in information under their own privacy policies.

Sharing and security

Your account information and reminders are intended to be private to your account. We do not share them except with service providers needed to operate the service, when required by law, or to protect the service and its users. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may sign out at any time. You may request correction or deletion of your account information by contacting us at jhurdich@gmail.com.

Children

Easy Reminder System is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to jhurdich@gmail.com.
